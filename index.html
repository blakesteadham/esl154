What's on the exam

The ESL Supplemental (154) measures the knowledge required to teach English learners in Texas public schools. It's a computer-administered, selected-response exam.

Domains · 9 competencies

Domain I — Language Concepts & Language Acquisition

≈ 25% of exam · Competencies 001–002

Fundamental linguistics (phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, pragmatics) and how first and second languages are acquired — Krashen, Cummins (BICS/CALP), stages of acquisition, interlanguage, transfer.

Domain II — ESL Instruction & Assessment

≈ 45% of exam · Competencies 003–006

The heavyweight domain. ESL methods (sheltered instruction, TPR, communicative approaches), teaching listening/speaking/reading/writing, ELPS integration, content-area support, and assessment — including TELPAS and formative/summative/authentic assessment.

Domain III — Foundations, Culture, Family & Community

≈ 30% of exam · Competencies 007–009

Landmark court cases (Lau, Castañeda, Plyler), federal and Texas law (Title VI, TEC Chapter 89), LPAC responsibilities, program models, multicultural awareness, and family/community engagement.

Study tip: the exam loves classroom scenarios — most questions describe a student or situation and ask for the best teacher response. When two answers seem right, pick the one that keeps instruction comprehensible, additive (values the home language), and appropriate to the student's proficiency stage. Verify current exam logistics at tx.nesinc.com before test day.
